## Summary

[pkft](https://github.com/buchanankerswell/pkft) finds peaks in one-dimensional datasets, such as groups of ages that each come with an uncertainty. Upload or paste in a small table of values, visualize the data, and fit peaks.

---

![Figure](figure.svg)

---

## Features

- Paste or import a table of values with uncertainties and explore the plots
- Fit peaks with a few adjustable settings, or let the app pick the peak count automatically
- Download the peak results as a file you can open in a spreadsheet
